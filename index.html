Set it up once. It runs itself.

Questions

You probably want to know...

What documents does FleetReady track?
COF (Certificate of Fitness), PrDP (Professional Driver's Permit), operating licenses, roadworthy certificates, and driver's licenses. Basically anything with an expiry date on your fleet.
How does the renewal admin work on Pro?
When a document is getting close to expiry, we book the NATIS appointment, fill in the forms, and send your driver a WhatsApp with exactly what to bring and where to go. You don't have to lift a finger.
Does my driver need to download anything?
No. Your drivers just get WhatsApp messages. No app to download, no logins. You manage everything from your dashboard.
How long does it take to get set up?
About 3 minutes per vehicle. You add the vehicle, upload the document, and FleetReady does the rest.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel any time straight from your account.
